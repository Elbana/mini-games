How to Farm

  1. Tap an empty plot and pick a seed to plant.
  2. When a badge appears, give the crop what it needs: 💧 water, 🌿 fertilizer, or 🐛 insecticide.
  3. Complete all care rounds, then tap a ⭐ ready plot to harvest.
  4. Sell crops at the Black Market from the hub.
  5. If a crop dies (💀), tap the plot once to clear it and plant again.

Badges only show when you can act — water, fertilizer, or insecticide.
