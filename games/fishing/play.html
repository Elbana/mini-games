How to Play

  1. Tap a bait chip at the bottom to equip it. Use + to buy more.
  2. Tap the open sea to cast — not the boat or HUD.
  3. Wait for the bite callout, then the reel fight begins.
  4. Hold anywhere to reel in. Release when the fish pulls hard.
  5. Keep the bobber in the green zone and fill the progress bar to land the fish.
  6. Sell your catch at the Black Market Exchange — open it in a new tab from the hub or after a catch.
